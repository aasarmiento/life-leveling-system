Questions, answered

Is Questify free?

Yes. Create an account and start logging right away. There is nothing to pay for.
Your entries are saved in your browser, so no server is involved.

Where is my data stored?

Everything is saved in your browser on this device. Nothing is sent to a server.

What counts as effort?

Any real work, learning, or health activity you choose to log.

Do I need an account?

Yes. A free account keeps your logs, XP, and level together.

Can I use it if I’m not a student?

Yes. Work, learning, and health all count, so it fits any routine.
